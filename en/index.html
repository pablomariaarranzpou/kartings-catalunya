Before you choose: engine, track and season

Engines differ

Most adult rental karts are 270 cc Hondas. Circuit d'Osona gives you a 390 cc for the same price (20 € for 10 min), El Pla a 400 cc Racing kart for 25 € and Calafat a 420 cc Racing kart. Nürburgreen is fully electric.

So do tracks

Indoor tracks (Penedès 600 m, Tarraco 600 m) have short, technical laps. Big outdoor tracks such as El Pla (1,400 m) or Vendrell (1,310 m) give more speed per minute, so 10 laps are not worth the same everywhere.

Watch the season

Palamós and L'Estartit close in winter, Altafulla only opens at weekends outside summer, and Salou and Calafat charge more in high season.

Check before you go

Almost no site dates its prices or says whether VAT is included, and several contradict themselves. Call or message to confirm the price, the offer and the minimum group size.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest go-kart track in Catalonia?

It depends how you measure it. Per minute on a single session, Scratch Indoor Karting Penedès (Vilafranca) charges €19 for 10 min, €1.90/min. With a pack, Karting El Pla (Alt Urgell) goes down to €1.20/min with 6 sessions for €72. In euros, the cheapest session with a published length is at Karting Minilandia (Viladecans): €16.90 for 7 min.

Where is there indoor karting near Barcelona?

Indoor Karting Barcelona (Sant Feliu de Llobregat, €21 for 8 min), Gené Karting (Viladecans, from €18 for 8 min), Nürburgreen Indoor, electric (Les Franqueses del Vallès, €22 for 8 min) and Scratch Indoor Karting Penedès (Vilafranca, €19 for 10 min).

How much is a go-kart race with qualifying?

Between €28 and €60 per person. The cheapest are the Penedès Mini Race (€28, minimum 5), the Sallent GP F3 (€30, minimum 4, Wednesday to Friday) and the Altafulla Fast Race GP (€30, minimum 6).

How many people do you need for a go-kart race?

Usually 6 to 10. The lowest minimums are Sallent (4), Penedès (5) and Tarraco's Fast GP offer (4). Only Zona Karting (6 to 10) and Karting Cross Empuriabrava (7 to 30) publish a maximum.

How long is a go-kart session?

7 to 10 minutes. The longest single sessions on sale are at Karting L'Escala (up to 18 min) and Karting Cross Empuriabrava (14 min).

How the data was collected

  1. All rental go-kart tracks in Catalonia were searched for in directories (Yumping, gokarting.es), tourism portals, regional press and searches in Spanish and Catalan, then cross-checked with a sweep for indoor and electric tracks.
  2. Prices come from each track's official website (price pages, online shops, price-list PDFs and images), checked on 25 November 2026.
  3. Price per minute is the published price divided by the published minutes on track. Races measured in laps have no €/min.
  4. Aggregator data, old price lists and contradictory figures are flagged and kept out of the rankings. Nothing is invented: if something was not found, it says “not published”.

Flags

  • no year The site does not say which year the prices are for.
  • conflict The same site gives two different figures.
  • old prices Price list from previous years.
  • seasonal Opening or prices change with the season.
  • unconfirmed Could not confirm it is open in late 2026.
  • third-party From an aggregator, not the official site.
  • search snippet Figure read from a search-engine snippet, not the page itself.
  • VAT incl. The site says prices include VAT.
